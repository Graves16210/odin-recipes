Vegetarian Mousaak Recipe

This Vegetarian Moussaka recipe is based on am authentic dish, made healthier. Layers of roasted eggplants, zucchini, and potatoes are layerd with a homemade lential tomato sauce, topped with bechamel sauce and sprinkled with cheese.

Ingedients

Vegetables:

  • 4 zucchini
  • 3 russet potatoes
  • 2 eggplant
  • 3 Tbsp dired oregano

Tomato Sauce:

  • 4 Tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 onion diced
  • 4 garlic cloves diced
  • 1 1/2 cups cooked lentils
  • 3 cups diced red tomatoes
  • 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper

bechamel Sauce:

  • 3 Tbsp butter
  • 3 Tbsp all purpose flour
  • 2 cups milk
  • 2 eggs

Instructions:

  1. Preheat oven to 5ooF. Prep and grease two baking sheets. Lightly grease and prep casserole dish.
  2. Slice zucchini, eggplants and potatoes.
  3. Spread out the vegetables in a single layer across the baking sheets. Drizzle with olive oil, and sprinkle lightly with oregano, saly and black pepper.
  4. Place baking sheets in the oven for 15 minutes. Eggplants with need an additional 5 minutes. Remove vegetables from baking sheets and set aside
  5. Set a large pot on the oven to medium heat and add 2 Tbsp of olive oil.
  6. Add oinions until softened.
  7. Add garlic for another minute
  8. Add cooked lentils, diced tomatoes. 1 Tbsp dried oregano, chili falkes, salt and pepper to taste
  9. Cook lentils in saucde for 10-12 minutes
  10. Add butter to a small sauce pan. Whisk in flour and cook until a paste.
  11. Add hot milk and whisk until sauce thickens. Bring sauce to boil than season with salt and pepper and cook, whisking constantly, for 2 to 3 minutes
  12. Whisk eggs into a medium bowl, then add a little of the sauce to the bowl: stir to combine. Pour the eggs into the sauce and let it cook for another 1-2 minutes.
  13. Assemble the Vegetarian Moussaka into a 9 x 13 baking dish.
  14. Pour 1/2 cip the lentil sauce into the bottom of the baking dish.
  15. Layer the eggplant on top. Then follow with a layer of the zucchini and potatoes. Sprinkle wit feta cheese and the remainder if the lential sauce. Reapt vegetables in the same order
  16. Pour in the bechamel sauce and smooth with a spatial. Top with the remaining feta and parmesan. Sprinkle with oregano
  17. Bake at 375 for 45 minutes. In the last 5 minutes switch to broil to brown cheese.
  18. Serve hot fpr best reslits
