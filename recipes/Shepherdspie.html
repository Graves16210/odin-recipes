Shepherds Pie

Classic Shepherds Pie

This Classic Shepherds pie is made with ground beef (of lamb) with vegetables in a rich gravy, topped with cheesy mashed potatoes and baked.

Ingredients

Meat Filling:

  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 cup chopped onion
  • 1 lb lean ground beef or lamb
  • 2 Tsp dried parsley
  • 1 Tsp dried rosemary
  • 1 Tsp dried thyme
  • 1/2 Tsp salt
  • 1/2 Tsp black pepper
  • 1 Tbsp Worcestershire Sauce
  • 2 minced garlic cloves
  • 2 Tbsp all purpose flour
  • 2 Tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 cup beef broth
  • 1 cup frozen mixed peas & carrots
  • 1/2 cup frozen corn kernels
  • 1 1/2 - 2 lb russet potatoes
  • 8 Tbs unsalted butter
  • 1/3 cup half & half
  • 1/2 Tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 Tsp salt
  • 1/3 Tsp black pepper
  • 1/4 cup parmesan cheese

Instructions:

Make The Meat Filling

  1. Add oil to a large skillet over med-high heat for 2 minutes. Add onoins and cook for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the ground beef (or gound lamd) to the skillet and beark it apart. Add parsley, rosemary, thyme, salt and pepper. Mix well and cook until meat is browned.
  3. Add the Worcestershire sauce and garlic, stire to combine cooking for 1 minute.
  4. Add flour and tomato paste, stir to incorporate.
  5. Add broth, peas, carrots and corn. Bring the liquid to a boil then reduce to simmer, roughly 5 mintues.
  6. Set the mixture aside and preheat oven to 400F

Make the Potato Topping

  1. Cut the potatoes into smaller pieces and add to a large pot. Cover with water and bring the water to a boil. Cook until potatoes are fork tender.
  2. Drain the water from the potatoes.
  3. Add butter, half & half, garlic powder, salt and pepper. Mash the potatoes and str until all ingredients aremixed together.
  4. Add parmesan cheese and stire to combine.

Assembly

  1. Pour the meat mixture into a 9x9 inch baking dish. Spread mixture to make an even layer. Spoon the potatoes on of the meat and spread to make an even layer.
  2. Baked uncovered for 25-30 mintues, cool for 15 mintues before serving.
